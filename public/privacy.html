Privacy Policy

Ethiopian Textbooks & EUEE Hub (Grades 9–12)
Effective Date: September 25, 2026 • Last Updated: September 25, 2026
Overview & Student Privacy Guarantee: Ethiopian Textbooks & EUEE Hub is a free, privately developed educational application created to provide Ethiopian secondary and preparatory students and teachers with offline access to curriculum study materials and examination prep. We respect student privacy and are committed to transparent, minimal data practices.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in privacy by default. We do not collect or store sensitive personal information:

  • We do not require user registration, accounts, or passwords to access textbooks or study tools.
  • We do not collect real names, phone numbers, home addresses, or national identity numbers.
  • We do not track your precise GPS location.
  • We do not access your phone's personal contacts, gallery, microphone, or SMS messages.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To enable 100% offline study capability, the app stores the following data strictly on your local device (using Android local storage and IndexedDB):

  • Downloaded Textbooks & Guides: PDF files and chapters you explicitly choose to save for offline access.
  • Study Progress & Bookmarks: Your reading position, marked favorite pages, and personal study notes.
  • Practice Exam Results: Locally saved scores from your Grade 12 EUEE practice quizzes so you can track your study performance over time.
  • App Preferences: Your selected theme (Dark or Light mode) and language settings.

This data stays completely on your personal device and can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's cache or storage.

3. Network Communications & Cloud Services

When internet connectivity is available, the app communicates with secure cloud endpoints (Google Firebase and Google Cloud):

  • To check for curriculum textbook catalogue updates and official examination announcements.
  • To deliver optional push notifications regarding new academic releases and exam schedules (if push notification permission is granted).
  • Standard server connection logs (such as IP address and request timestamps) are automatically generated for security and CDN caching purposes.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services

To keep the platform accessible to students free of charge, the app may display standardized banner or interstitial educational advertisements via verified ad networks (such as Google AdMob). These services may collect pseudonymous device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) in strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and COPPA regulations.

5. Children & Student Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is designed for secondary school and preparatory high school students (Grades 9 to 12). We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or monetize personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that information has been provided to us inadvertently, please contact us immediately, and we will delete it promptly.

6. Educational Content & Intellectual Property Notice

Ethiopian Textbooks & EUEE Hub is an independent educational tool. All curriculum frameworks, textbook syllabuses, and official examination papers remain the intellectual property of the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia Ministry of Education (MOE) and respective regional education bureaus. Materials are provided for non-commercial reference and student study support.

7. Data Deletion & User Rights

You have full control over your data. To completely delete all local data, notes, and downloaded books:

  • Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Ethiopian Textbooks → Storage and tap Clear Data / Clear Storage.
  • Or uninstall the application from your device.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published directly on this permanent URL with an updated effective date.
